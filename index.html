A few things to know.

Does KeyHarbour hold or insure my crypto?

No. You keep control of your funds and keys. KeyHarbour provides safety checks and educational guidance. It does not hold funds, insure losses or stop transactions.

Will you need my recovery phrase or wallet connection?

No. Never give your recovery phrase, private key or PIN to this service or a person claiming to represent it. These tools require no wallet connection or signature.

Does the check prove my wallet is secure?

No. The assessment covers habits you report; it does not inspect your wallet or device. The link checker uses a threat list and basic address checks. Unlisted links, compromised websites and malicious signatures can still cause harm.

Are you part of Ledger or MetaMask?

No. KeyHarbour is an independent project. We cite their public guidance and attribute the phishing-list data. No partnership or endorsement is implied.
