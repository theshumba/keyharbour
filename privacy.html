Privacy and product limitations · Updated 14 October 2026.

What these tools collect

The assessment uses eight multiple-choice answers about your habits. It does not ask for your name, email, wallet address, balances, PIN, recovery phrase, private key or backup location. There are no accounts, marketing forms or analytics in this release.

Optional local saving

Your answers stay in memory unless you choose “Keep my checklist in this browser”. If you choose to save, the answers and their saved date are stored in localStorage on this browser and website origin. They are not encrypted. Other people with access to the browser may be able to read them. On GitHub Pages, other apps served from the same github.io origin may also be able to read this storage.

Switch saving off or select “Clear my checklist” to remove the stored checklist. Downloaded plans and calendar files are separate files you control; clearing the checklist does not remove them. A shared device is a reason to leave saving off.

Checking a link

The entered URL is processed locally. The checker never opens the submitted site or sends that URL to a screening provider. It uses the hostname and path to match a phishing dataset. Query parameters, fragments and credentials are not shown in results or retained by the app. Do not paste secret information into the input.

A bundled MetaMask phishing-list snapshot is loaded from this site's hosting. “Refresh threat list” retrieves a list revision from api.github.com and the corresponding file from raw.githubusercontent.com. These providers see your IP address and ordinary network metadata, not the URL you are checking. If refresh fails, the app keeps the bundled snapshot and shows its date. Lists older than 48 hours are marked out of date.

Website hosting and external links

GitHub Pages hosts this site and may process request logs under GitHub’s privacy statement. External support and guidance links take you to other providers with their own policies. There is no advertising or cookie-based tracking added by KeyHarbour.

What the results mean

The safety plan records self-reported habits. It does not inspect your wallet, device, approvals, account access or funds. It is not a security certification, risk probability or professional incident-response service.

The link checker detects matches in its loaded list and a few visible address warning signs. It cannot guarantee detection of new scams, redirects, compromised official pages, unsafe contracts, signatures or content. “No list match found” and “Recognised website name” both leave risk unresolved. Do not treat either as permission to connect or sign.

Funds and support

KeyHarbour does not hold or transfer funds, execute transactions, revoke approvals, insure losses or recover stolen crypto. It offers educational first steps and links to third-party guidance. No paid service or staffed support is offered in this release. Never transfer money to someone who claims to be KeyHarbour support.
