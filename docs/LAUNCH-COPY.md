# KeyHarbour launch copy

## Short introduction

KeyHarbour helps everyday crypto holders build safer wallet habits. Check your setup, screen a suspicious link and make a personal safety plan, without connecting a wallet or sharing recovery words.

## Launch post

Buying a hardware wallet is only part of the job.

You still need to protect the recovery phrase, understand what you sign and know what to do when a message looks wrong.

I built KeyHarbour to make those moments clearer. It gives you a short wallet-habit check, a personal action plan, a link checker using MetaMask’s published phishing list and first steps for common emergencies.

You keep your keys. There is no wallet connection, account or signature request.

It cannot guarantee safety or recover stolen funds. It can help you see what needs your attention before your next decision.

Try it at https://theshumba.github.io/keyharbour/
