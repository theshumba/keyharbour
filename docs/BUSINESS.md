# KeyHarbour

**Promise:** Know what to check before you put your crypto at risk.

**First customer:** An existing crypto holder with a Ledger or another hardware wallet who wants practical help with setup, suspicious messages and recovery preparation. This is a customer hypothesis, not verified demand.

**Problem:** Owning a hardware wallet still leaves people responsible for the recovery phrase, the details they approve and their response to a scam. Ledger's own guidance describes these responsibilities [1].

**Position:** The plain-language safety desk around the wallet. The customer keeps control. KeyHarbour helps them make safer decisions and follow through on neglected security habits.

## Offer and revenue

The launched tools are available without an account: a wallet-habit assessment, domain screening, emergency guidance and a downloadable plan.

Test a £49 one-off guided setup session as the first paid offer. Deliver a 45-minute walkthrough of the customer's own setup, without screen-sharing recovery phrases, remotely controlling their wallet or handling transactions. Supply a checklist afterwards. This price and duration are hypotheses, not a live purchasable service.

If customers ask for continuing help, test a £79 annual household safety review: annual setup review, a household recovery drill and updated educational guidance. Do not sell this as continuous monitoring. Avoid a subscription until there is repeat value.

At £49 and an assumed 60 minutes of total delivery/admin time, revenue per founder-hour is £49 before fees, tax, overheads and acquisition costs. This is arithmetic based on an assumption, not evidence of margin or demand. No market-size or revenue forecast is claimed.

## Why this can be distinct

Ledger publishes security education and sells hardware [1]. Revoke.cash specialises in approval inspection and revocation [3]. MetaMask maintains phishing-domain data [2]. KeyHarbour combines practical explanations with a repeatable personal safety routine. It must prove that users value the guided experience enough to return or pay; reusing a public list alone is not a defensible business.

## Customer acquisition

Start with educational demonstrations: spotting a support impersonator, verifying an address on the device, and distinguishing a lost device from an exposed phrase. Publish the useful tool first, then interview ten existing hardware-wallet owners. These are proposed activities, not completed outreach.

Interview prompts: what made you buy the wallet; what still worries you; what did you last find confusing; what have you paid for help; would you book this specific £49 session now? Do not solicit wallet balances, keys or screenshots of recovery materials.

Measure assessment completion, whether the recommended action was understood, repeat use of the link tool and willingness to book a paid walkthrough. Begin with consenting interviews; the launched app contains no analytics.

## Operating rules

Never ask for a seed phrase, private key, passphrase, PIN or transfer to a supposed safe wallet. Never connect to or control the customer's wallet. Never claim Ledger affiliation, FCA approval, reimbursement or guaranteed safety. Never offer to recover stolen money for an upfront fee.

Do not offer custody, trading, investment recommendations, insurance or transaction execution. Obtain qualified advice about the actual business perimeter before opening paid services. FCA registration requirements depend on the services provided; this document does not conclude that the business is exempt [4].

For an incident, show educational triage and official support routes. Do not treat a checklist result as proof that funds are safe. Do not top up an account suspected of being controlled by a sweeper bot [3].

## Owner decisions before selling

- Clear the working name through appropriate company and trademark checks. A web search is not clearance.
- Decide the legal entity, business inbox and customer-support hours.
- Get the paid walkthrough's terms, consumer cancellation rules, data handling and regulatory perimeter reviewed for the actual service.
- Choose a payment account and enable checkout only when the service can be delivered.
- Buy a domain if desired. No domain has been bought during this build.
