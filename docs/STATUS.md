## Before selling a paid service

The £49 guided setup session is a proposal, not a live booking service. The owner still needs name clearance, legal/entity decisions, reviewed service terms and regulatory perimeter, a business inbox, delivery capacity and a payment account. No company, trademark or custom domain was registered, and no checkout or staffed support is advertised.

The released tools help users reduce risk through habits and known-threat checks. They do not autonomously block transactions, monitor wallets, insure funds or recover stolen crypto. Hardware-device connection and native-phone device tests are inapplicable to this browser-only release; no such capability is claimed.

For updates, edit main, run the relevant checks, refresh the dataset when appropriate and publish only public/ to the pages branch. README.md explains the commands. Do not force-push or introduce wallet secrets into any form or stored record.
